Food · Gym · Shop privacy policy

This is a personal app. Your food log, diet, workouts and profile are stored on your phone and in your own Supabase account.

Health Connect data

The app reads only your step count from Health Connect, and only after you allow it. Steps per day are kept on your phone and used to work out how many calories you burned that day, so your daily calorie target fits what you actually did.

Step data is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. The app doesn't write anything to Health Connect.

You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect's settings.
